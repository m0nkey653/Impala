Atlas Viewer

Open folder… and pick the repo root to list every atlas in it, or drop an atlas's .xml and .png here together.

Folder scans skip .git, build, out, .vs and node_modules. Chrome/Edge scan quickly; other browsers ask to “upload” the folder (nothing leaves your machine).

Reads the Starling/Sparrow <TextureAtlas><SubTexture name x y width height/> format used by Kenney sprite sheets (e.g. games/dzone/assets/sprites/kenney-space-shooter/sheet.xml).
